DISTRIBUTION-DISTANCE METRICS (three, deliberately separate)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The three distances are NOT comparable to each other in magnitude -- each
  is a Frechet distance computed in a DIFFERENT embedding space, so only
  relative comparisons WITHIN one metric (e.g. donor vs acceptor, or
  approach vs approach) are meaningful.
    FP-PCA-FD = 25.230 : distance in a 2-component PCA projection of
        Morgan-fingerprint space. This is the metric the earlier reports
        mislabelled as 'Frechet Distance'. Only 2 PCs are retained, so it
        captures the coarsest structural drift and ignores everything the
        first two components do not explain. Lower = closer to training.
    PFD       = 444.259 : PRIMARY domain-appropriate distance. Computed in
        the penultimate activation space of a property model trained on
        THIS dataset's OPV donors/acceptors, so the axes correspond to
        features that actually predict device-relevant properties. Its
        absolute scale is arbitrary (set by the model's activations), so
        use it comparatively, never as an absolute quality number.
    FCD       = 26.515 : real Frechet ChemNet Distance. CAVEAT: ChemNet is
        trained on ChEMBL bioactivity, i.e. drug-like space. OPV
        donors/acceptors are out of that domain, so this measures drug-
        likeness-space distance. Reported ONLY for cross-field
        comparability with the generative-chemistry literature.

DESCRIPTOR-DOMAIN AGREEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  legacy_property_match_mean = 0.9187
    Definition: fraction of 6 RDKit descriptors (MW, LogP, TPSA, HBA,
      HBD, RotBonds) falling inside the training min-max interval.
    LIMITATION: it counts binary in/out conditions, so it can only
      take discrete values separated by 1/6 = 0.167 -- which is why
      its histogram shows sharp spikes rather than a smooth spread.
      It says nothing about frontier-orbital energies, absorption or
      device properties. Retained only for continuity with the older
      reports; prefer the two distribution-aware measures below.
    Interpretation: on average 5.51 of the 6 descriptors stay inside
      the training ranges.

  wasserstein_norm_mean = 1.5644
    Definition: mean per-descriptor 1-Wasserstein distance between the
      generated and training descriptor distributions, normalised by
      the training spread. Distribution-aware, continuous, and it
      does not collapse to discrete steps the way the 6-condition
      count above does. Lower = the generated descriptor
      distributions track the training ones more closely.

  mahalanobis_gen_mean = 447.9467
    Definition: mean Mahalanobis distance of generated molecules to
      the training descriptor manifold, which accounts for descriptor
      CORRELATION instead of treating each axis independently. Large
      values mean the pool sits outside the correlated descriptor
      envelope the downstream surrogate was fitted on -- i.e. those
      predictions are extrapolations and should be trusted less.

    TIER R1 AiZynthFinder MCTS route search : real retrosynthesis
            evidence. Only molecules audited with this backend may be
            described as having a found route.
    TIER R2 RAscore : a classifier distilled FROM AiZynthFinder outcomes,
            so it approximates 'is a route findable' in milliseconds.
            Its own authors state it does not transfer outside its ChEMBL
            training domain, and these OPV scaffolds are outside it.
            Indicative only.
    TIER R3 SAScore / TANGO-inspired heuristic : structural complexity and
            building-block overlap. Fast screens. Neither is a probability
            that a synthesis route exists, and the TANGO-inspired score is
            NOT the published retrosynthesis-constrained TANGO framework.
