METRIC NAMING (corrections applied, stated explicitly)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Internal Diversity (IntDiv)      = 1 - mean pairwise Tanimoto.
      Previously mislabelled 'Mean Inception Score'. It is NOT an
      inception score (no classifier, no softmax, no KL divergence);
      it is the MOSES-standard internal-diversity statistic.
  FP-PCA-FD                        = Frechet distance in a 2-component PCA
      projection of Morgan-fingerprint space. Previously reported as
      'Frechet Distance'. It is NOT Frechet ChemNet Distance.
  PFD (Property-Embedding Frechet) = Frechet distance in the penultimate
      activation space of a property model trained on THIS domain
      (OPV donors/acceptors). PRIMARY domain-appropriate distance.
  FCD (Frechet ChemNet Distance)   = the real thing, via the `fcd` package.
      CAVEAT: ChemNet is ChEMBL-bioactivity-trained, so this measures
      distance in a drug-likeness embedding, not an OPV-relevant one.
      Reported for cross-field comparability only.
  TANGO-inspired heuristic         = building-block-overlap screen. NOT the
      published retrosynthesis-constrained TANGO framework, and NOT a
      probability that a synthesis route exists.
